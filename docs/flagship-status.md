## Governance

- Prefer focused utilities over overlapping platforms.
- Require evidence of practical value before expanding scope.
- Treat tests as engineering evidence, not proof of professional adoption.
- Keep observed facts, inferences, official projections and hypothetical scenarios separate.
- No consolidation or further repository deletion without an explicit decision.
- Do not expose personal, customer or employer data.
